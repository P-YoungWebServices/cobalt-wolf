Requests with Seerr

Cobalt Wolf integrates with Seerr (Overseerr/Jellyseerr-compatible) so everyone on the server can discover and request media without leaving the app — and admins can approve requests from the couch.

Connecting Seerr

  • Enter your Seerr server's URL in service settings.
  • Sign in with your Jellyfin account — the same credentials you used for the server.
  • The session is kept in encrypted storage; the app reports its state as Checking, Signed in, Signed out or Not configured.
Seerr is optional. If none is configured, the Seerr sections simply don't appear — the rest of the app is unaffected.

Discovering & requesting

  • Browse trending and popular movies and series.
  • Search once, get both: the app's search runs Jellyfin and Seerr queries together, showing "On this server" and "From Seerr" sections on one page.
  • Request a movie or a series — for shows you can pick which seasons to request.
  • Requests respect your Seerr permissions, quotas and approval requirements.

Tracking your requests

  • My requests lists everything you've asked for with its status: pending, approved, available or declined.
  • Local notifications tell you when a request is approved or becomes available — a watcher polls while you're signed in.

The approval queue (admins)

  • Admins get a dedicated request queue destination.
  • Approve or decline pending requests one by one, in bulk or as a queue.
  • On TV it sits under the Server dashboard's management section and is fully D-pad navigable.
