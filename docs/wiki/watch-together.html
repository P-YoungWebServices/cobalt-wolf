Watch together

Cobalt Wolf supports Jellyfin SyncPlay — groups that keep play, pause and seek synchronized across everyone's devices in real time.

Starting a group

There's no navigation entry for SyncPlay — it starts from the content:

  • Open any movie or episode's detail page.
  • Tap Watch together.
  • Create a new group or join an existing one. The item you came from is queued for the whole group.

How sync works

  • One shared transport: anyone's play, pause or seek applies to the group.
  • Your device reports its position and buffering state back, so the group waits rather than drifting apart.
  • Works across phone, tablet and TV — everyone can be on different devices.
  • The group lives independently of screens: navigating away doesn't drop your membership.

Good to know

  • Auto-advance is off in groups — episodes don't auto-play the next one, so nobody gets left behind. Use the group's controls to queue the next item.
  • Same item, different versions — each member plays the media source that works for their device; sync is by position, not by bytes.
  • Leave anytime — leaving the group returns your player to normal local control.
  • SyncPlay requires all members to be on the same Jellyfin server.
