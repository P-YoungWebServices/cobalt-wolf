Offline downloads

Save movies and episodes to your phone or tablet and watch them without a connection — on a plane, in the car, anywhere your server can't reach.

Downloading

  • Open the detail page for a movie or episode and choose Download.
  • Downloads appear in the app's Downloads section, where you can watch progress, pause or remove them.
  • Downloaded items play through the same player — the full playback engine, subtitle support and track pickers included.
Downloads are available on phone and tablet. On TV, the Downloads entry is an admin-only route used for the qBittorrent monitor — see Calendar & media manager.

Managing storage

  • Downloads live in the app's private storage — they don't clutter your gallery and are removed if the app is uninstalled.
  • Delete items individually from the Downloads section when you're done to reclaim space.
  • Keep an eye on device storage before grabbing large files: a 4K remux can be tens of gigabytes.

Don't confuse this with the qBittorrent monitor

The admin Downloads route on TV and the torrent monitor are a different feature: they show your server's qBittorrent transfers, not files on this device. Offline downloads covered here are media saved to this device from your Jellyfin library.
